THE FIRST SESSION

Three checks before your first payment.

01

Use one public handle

Put the same payment name in your overlay, channel panels, bio, and chat command. Do not make viewers guess which account is yours.

02

Confirm before money moves

Tell people to verify the recipient profile and amount before sending. Familiar display names can be copied or mistyped.

03

Keep private records

Track tips, moderator payments, and giveaways away from public chat. A payment handle is not a bookkeeping system.

THE NON-NEGOTIABLE RULE

A payment should never require a login code.

  • Never post a phone number or email used for account recovery.
  • Never ask a winner to pay money to “unlock” a giveaway prize.
  • Keep support messages and refund requests outside your sign-in flow.
