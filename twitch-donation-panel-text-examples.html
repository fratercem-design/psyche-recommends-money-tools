THE FORMULA

Tell people what the link does before they click.

A useful Twitch support panel has four parts: a short title, the purpose of the payment, the verified destination, and one safety instruction. The payment path should take less space than the explanation of what viewers are supporting.

  1. Name the action.

    “Support the stream” and “Send a tip” are clear. “Click here” makes the viewer guess.

  2. Name the destination.

    Say Cash App, another payment provider, or your approved tip page. Do not disguise an external payment link as a Twitch feature.

  3. Explain what support funds.

    A line about equipment, art supplies, community events, or production costs gives the panel a reason to exist.

  4. Add a safety line.

    Ask viewers to confirm the profile name and amount. State that you will never request a PIN or sign-in code.

Twitch allows a panel title, image, linked image, and description with Markdown links. Read Twitch’s current panel instructions.

COPY EXAMPLES

Choose the version that matches the channel.

01 / PLAIN SUPPORT

Support the stream

Tips help cover stream equipment and production costs. Use the payment link below, then confirm the profile name and amount before sending.

02 / CASH APP

Send a tip with Cash App

Cash App: $yourhandle. Confirm the recipient profile before sending. I will never ask for your PIN, password, or sign-in code.

03 / CHANNEL GOAL

Help fund the next upgrade

Current goal: a new microphone for the stream. Tips are optional and do not purchase special treatment or a guaranteed response.

04 / ART OR MUSIC

Support the work behind the stream

Tips help pay for materials, software, and production time. Please check the recipient name and amount before you approve a payment.

05 / COMMUNITY EVENTS

Support community nights

Optional tips help cover prizes and event costs. Giveaway winners are never asked to pay money to receive a prize.

06 / LOW-PRESSURE

Want to support the channel?

Watching and sharing are already support. If you want to leave a tip, use the verified link below and check every detail before sending.

Replace the placeholders: write your actual channel purpose and paste the public payment destination you verified inside the provider’s app. Do not guess a web address from a Cashtag.

CHAT VERSION

Keep the bot command shorter than the panel.

A bot command should repeat the destination and the most important safety line. It should not interrupt chat every few minutes.

Support the stream: [verified payment link]. Confirm the recipient profile and amount before sending. Never share a PIN or sign-in code.

If you use Cash App, our Twitch Cash App setup guide includes a browser-only generator that writes this text from your channel name and public Cashtag.

WORDING CHECK

Do not promise what the payment cannot deliver.

  • Avoid “donation” if the payment is not charitable or tax-deductible.
  • Do not promise a giveaway win, shout-out, or service you cannot guarantee.
  • Do not say a Cash App panel creates Twitch alerts or Twitch payout records.
  • Do not call a third-party payment link an official Twitch payment feature.
  • Do not put private account-recovery details in your panel or bot command.
  • Do not tell viewers that every payment can be reversed or refunded.
