BEFORE YOU OPEN

Questions worth answering first.

Does Ally cover up to $100 immediately?

No. Ally says that in most cases basic CoverDraft eligibility begins 30 days after a total of $100 has been deposited into an eligible Spending Account. Account eligibility and transaction approval still apply.

Can coverage increase to $250?

Ally says expanded coverage may become available after qualifying direct deposits of at least $250 for two consecutive months. At least one qualifying direct deposit every 45 days is generally required to maintain the expanded option.

What happens after the account goes negative?

Future deposits automatically apply to the negative balance. Ally generally gives you 14 days to restore the balance above zero; read the current agreement for the consequences and exact timing.

Is CoverDraft a loan or guaranteed overdraft?

No. Ally describes CoverDraft as a service, not a line of credit or a guarantee. A transaction may still be declined, and any negative balance must be repaid.
