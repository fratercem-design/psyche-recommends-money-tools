HOW WE WORK

Editorial and referral standards.

The rules behind every recommendation, factual claim, update date, and compensated link on this site.

01

Start with a real task

Each page should help a reader complete a recurring job before asking them to click a referral link. A copied product description is not enough.

02

Use primary provider sources

Eligibility, fees, limits, deadlines, exclusions, and referral terms are checked against current provider documentation. Relevant source links appear on the guide.

03

Qualify every financial claim

“Up to,” “if eligible,” “may,” and timing conditions stay attached to the claim they qualify. We do not promise approval, coverage, payment, savings, or a referral reward.

04

Put disclosures beside the action

A compensated referral is identified near the button, not hidden in a general footer. Sponsored destinations receive a rel="sponsored" link attribute.

05

Date the research, not the rewrite

A provider-review date changes only after the cited terms and product language are checked again. Cosmetic edits do not make financial information newly reviewed.

06

Keep editorial control

Providers do not approve these independent guides. Referral compensation does not buy a favorable conclusion or remove limitations from the page.

07

Correct the record

If current provider documentation conflicts with a guide, the provider documentation controls. Readers can propose corrections through the public repository.
